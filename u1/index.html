Grammar focus
  • Verbs of liking such as adore, love, like, enjoy, fancy, dislike, hate, detest, prefer are commonly followed by V-ing.
  • Some verbs may be followed by either V-ing or to + verb with little change in meaning: begin, bear, stand, continue, hate, like, love, prefer, propose, start.
  • Adding -ing: do → doing; live → living; lie → lying; run → running.

Mai's Weekend

Mai loves spending time outdoors. On Saturday mornings, she enjoys riding her bike around the lake with her sister. Her sister prefers walking, but she sometimes agrees to cycle with Mai. After lunch, Mai likes reading comics or watching short videos. She dislikes staying indoors all day, so in the afternoon she often starts doing her homework early and then goes outside. On Sundays, she loves to visit her grandparents. Her grandfather likes gardening and Mai enjoys helping him water the plants.
