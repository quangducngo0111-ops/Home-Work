5. Reading Practice

Đọc đoạn văn dưới đây. Các từ trong bài đều liên quan đến nhóm từ vựng vừa học.

Can you imagine living in a house that moves gently with the water? Can you imagine travelling to school by boat every day?

In her photo story Life on the Lake, photographer Emma Brooks travels from Melbourne to a floating village in Southeast Asia. She spends six weeks with the local people and learns about their traditional way of life.

The village is built on a large lake. There are no roads between the houses, so people travel by boat. Most families get up before sunrise. The men usually fish in the early morning and take their catch to the market later in the day.

Many women work at small shops or prepare food for their families. Some also repair fishing nets and help to sell fish at the market. The children travel to school by boat and often help their parents after class.

The whole community works together during the rainy season. When the water rises, families check their homes, repair boats and move important things to higher places. Emma says the village is very different from city life, but the people are friendly and work closely together.
